Troubleshooting

If a laboratory app takes time to open, Streamlit may be waking it from sleep. If a component fails to load, try a hard refresh or open the app in a different browser. For persistent problems, check the app logs in Streamlit Community Cloud.

Laboratory access passwords are entered only inside the individual applications, never on this public home page.
